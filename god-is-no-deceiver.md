# God is no deceiver

[God exists](./god-exists.md) as a supremely perfect being. And
[supreme perfection is incompatible with deception](./perfection-entails-truthfulness.md) —
deception is a defect, and a perfect being has no defects. Therefore, God
would never deceive me or arrange for my cognitive faculties to be
systematically unreliable.
